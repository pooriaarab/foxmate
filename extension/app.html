                    Saved logins (foxvault)

                    At a sign-in step, Chat can fill a saved login on its site. Each fill waits for your Approve, with the site and the field. foxvault keeps the password encrypted. The planner and the log get only its handle.

                      Rules (foxgate)

                      A rule is a standing answer for one site. "Always allow" on an approval card skips that step next time, but never after the goal read private data, never for a payment or a saved-login fill, and never on a lent login. "Always ask" and "Never allow" make foxmate stricter.

                        Notices (foxnotify)

                        When a goal needs you, ends, or a schedule runs late, and no sidebar is in view, foxmate shows a notice on this computer. A click opens the tab or the approval. It never approves.

                        Type an address to see the request.

                        Payments (foxpay)

                        foxmate can pay a bill on the tab's site with test USDC on Base Sepolia (x402). Each payment waits for your Approve, with the exact amount, payee and site. A cap of 0 turns payments off.

                        foxvault keeps the key encrypted. The planner never gets it.
