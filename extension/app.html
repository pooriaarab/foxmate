                    Model

                    Approval rules

                    A rule is a standing answer for one site. "Always allow" on an approval skips that step on that site next time. It is never offered for a payment, a saved-login fill, a lent login, or after a goal read private data. "Always ask" and "Never allow" make foxmate stricter.
